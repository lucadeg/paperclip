# MVX Marketing Evidence-Governed Council

**Template only; not a claim of live employment or human endorsement.** This team imports 57 executable Paperclip agent identities (27 executive decision frameworks and 30 marketing task owners). Each becomes runnable only after installation into a company with configured adapters and budgets. Model descriptions do not impersonate the real people referenced by the existing Sovereign Council UI.

All campaign, CRM, creative, provider, spend and conversion truth remains in MVX Growth OS (Tier 1). Paperclip (Tier 2) owns issue delegation, company identity, approvals, budget limits and audit. No independent shadow database.

## Non-negotiable operating protocol

1. Resolve tenant and company ID and valid authority before retrieving or sharing data. Default deny missing grants.
2. Link each assertion to a dated, tenant-bound Tier 1 evidence record with source and version. Reject fabricated telemetry.
3. Preregister baseline, hypothesis, primary KPI, guardrails, sample sizes, experiment horizon and decision rule before testing.
4. Specialists may analyze and prepare drafts using approved read scopes. Any publication, outreach, provider mutation or media spend requires Tier 1 scoped action API, explicit policy approval, budget reservation and receipt.
5. Council members deliberate only on domain-relevant tickets; critic-munger documents downside and independent challenge. Preserve disagreements and do not override legal/security or hard budget vetoes.
6. Evidence gaps produce blocked issues, not optimistic conclusions. Reconcile cost net of fees, VAT, refunds and discounts.
7. Paperclip tasks and recurring routines do not independently guarantee runtime activation: company install, adapter credentials, scoped provider connectors and heartbeat schedules are mandatory.
